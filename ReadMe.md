### About

I build systems where being wrong is expensive — financial ledgers, clinical records, retrieval that has to cite its sources honestly — and I ship the tests, audit trail and documentation alongside the feature rather than after it.

- 🏦 **Fintech** — double-entry ledger accounting, NAV and fee engines, cost-basis and realized-P&L attribution. Money arithmetic in bigint smallest-units with allocation that asserts conservation and fails closed.
- 🔎 **Applied AI & retrieval** — two production RAG systems. Hybrid retrieval fusing BM25 with dense vectors by reciprocal rank, and citation grounding designed so a model *cannot* fabricate a source.
- 🏥 **Health tech** — clinical practice management, structured extraction from medical documents, SNOMED CT coding, HL7 and health-information-exchange integration.
- 🌍 **Bilingual by default** — Arabic and English, full RTL. I treat i18n as a build-time guarantee, not a review-time hope.
- 🧱 **Architecture** — turning hard-coded process into data: declarative process engines, expression languages, plugin registries, and parity harnesses that prove a rewrite before it ships.
